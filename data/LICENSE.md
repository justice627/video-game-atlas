# Data licence

The tables in this folder are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Quotes in `links.csv` and excerpts in `evidence/` are short passages from the sources named next to them. They keep their sources' terms: Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 4.0, and every quote and excerpt carries its source URL. Coordinates and identifiers from Wikidata are CC0.

**Logo images are excluded.** The files in `logos/` are game and series logos from Wikimedia Commons. Each keeps its own Commons licence (public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA), listed with its author in `logos.csv`; see `logos/README.md`. They are trademarks of their owners.
